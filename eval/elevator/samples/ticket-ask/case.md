Ticket: Reduce failed name lookups after a cache refresh. The cache is the saved list that links names to user accounts. The implemented change fills it from the current directory and removes stale entries through the existing refresh path. In the fixed trial of 100 lookups after refresh, misses fell from 12 to 2. A miss means the requested name has no saved account. Both remaining misses were newly added users not yet included in a refresh: Alex and Sam. The ticket's acceptance criterion requires zero cache misses after the change. No cache can promise that: a user added after the refresh, like Alex, is absent until the next refresh. A proposed replacement criterion is at most 2 misses in this fixed 100-lookup trial, with the between-refresh gap stated explicitly; it is not a guarantee for all future traffic. The decision left to me is whether to ship with that rewritten miss-rate bound or push the ticket back. Explain the change and give your pick with one reason.
